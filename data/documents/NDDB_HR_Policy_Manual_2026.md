### Executive Preamble & Scope

The National Dairy Development Board (NDDB) promotes, finances, and supports producer-owned and controlled organizations. Ensuring operational continuity across critical dairy cold-chain infrastructure, national milk grid logistics, and enterprise Information & Communication Technology (ICT) systems requires a robust, transparent, and employee-centric governance framework. 

This document sets forth comprehensive rules governing leave entitlements, attendance regularization, statutory benefits, and employee self-service automation. All rules described herein are authoritative and serve as the deterministic reference for both departmental managers and enterprise AI agent copilots (e.g., HR Agentic RAG Routers).

### Section 1: General Leave Governance Framework

#### 1.1 Annual Leave Cycle
The official leave accounting year spans from **January 1 to December 31** of each calendar year. All leave allocations are credited on the first calendar day of January or prorated on a monthly basis for new entrants joining mid-year.

#### 1.2 Categories of Leave Entitlement
Permanent and contractual employees in active service are entitled to the following categorized leaves:
1. **Casual Leave (CL)** — For short, unforeseen personal exigencies.
2. **Sick & Medical Leave (SL)** — For personal illness, medical convalescence, or hospitalization.
3. **Earned / Privilege Leave (EL)** — For planned vacations, rest, and personal recreation.
4. **Bereavement & Compassionate Leave (BL)** — Paid leave for mourning the loss of immediate family members.
5. **Maternity / Paternity Leave (ML / PL)** — Statutory parenthood benefits.
6. **Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)** — Granted for operational duties executed on designated public holidays or weekly offs upon Chilling Center Manager / ICT Director sign-off.

#### 1.3 General Leave Principles
- **Leave is a Benefit, Not an Absolute Right**: Sanctioning of leave is contingent upon departmental workload, critical cold-chain shifts, and business exigencies.
- **Combination Rules**: Casual Leave (CL) cannot be prefixed, suffixed, or combined with Earned Leave (EL). Any continuous absence combining CL and EL shall result in the entire duration being converted and debited from the employee's Earned Leave balance.
- **Unauthorized Absence**: Absence from duty without an approved leave application or retroactive emergency regularization within 48 hours is treated as *dies non* (break in service) and triggers formal review.

### Section 2: Casual Leave (CL) Regulations

#### 2.1 Annual Allocation & Credit
- Permanent and full-time technical employees are allocated **twelve (12) working days** of Casual Leave per calendar year.
- Casual Leave is credited in full on January 1st of each calendar year.

#### 2.2 Application Bounds & Consecutive Days Limit
- **Maximum Consecutive Duration**: Casual Leave may be availed for a **maximum of three (3) consecutive working days** at any single instance.
- **Divisional Head Approval**: Taking four (4) or more consecutive working days of casual leave is strictly disallowed under standard self-service workflows. It mandates special prior dispensation and written approval from the respective **Divisional Head** (or Chief Information Officer for ICT personnel).
- **Public Holidays & Weekends**: Saturdays, Sundays, and gazetted national/state holidays falling within or adjoining the casual leave period do not count against the 12-day quota.

#### 2.3 Year-End Lapse & Non-Encashment
- Casual Leave balances **lapse entirely on December 31st** of each calendar year.
- Unused Casual Leave **cannot be encashed**, nor can it be accumulated or carried forward into subsequent calendar years.

---

### Section 3: Sick & Medical Leave (SL) Regulations

#### 3.1 Allocation & Accumulation
- Employees are entitled to **ten (10) working days** of Sick & Medical Leave on full pay for each calendar year.
- Unused Sick Leave may be accumulated up to a maximum ceiling of 180 working days across the employee's service tenure. Accumulated sick leave may be encashed exclusively at the time of superannuation or voluntary retirement in accordance with NDDB Retirement By-laws.

#### 3.2 Mandatory Medical Certificate Rule (`SEC-HR-3.2`)
- **Single or Two-Day Absence**: An employee reporting sick for one (1) or two (2) consecutive working days may submit a self-declaration through the NDDB Self-Service HR Portal upon resumption.
- **Duration Exceeding Two (2) Consecutive Days**: Any sick or medical leave extending beyond **two (2) consecutive working days** mandates a digital copy of a valid medical certificate issued and signed by a Registered Medical Practitioner (holding MBBS or equivalent qualification, registered with the National Medical Commission / State Medical Council).
- **Submission Timeline**: The medical certificate and fitness certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within **48 hours** of returning to active duty. Failure to submit documentation automatically converts the excess days into unauthorized leave without pay (LWP).

### Section 4: Bereavement & Compassionate Leave (BL) Regulations

#### 4.1 Objective & Scope
The National Dairy Development Board recognizes the emotional and logistical burdens accompanying bereavement. Compassionate leave is provided to ensure employees can mourn, perform last rites, and support family members without loss of compensation or leave balances.

#### 4.2 Policy Clause `SEC-HR-4.3`: Bereavement Entitlement
> **Clause `SEC-HR-4.3`**: In the unfortunate event of the demise of an **immediate family member**, an employee is granted **up to five (5) consecutive working days of paid Bereavement Leave**.

#### 4.3 Definition of "Immediate Family"
For the purposes of Clause `SEC-HR-4.3`, "Immediate Family" is strictly defined as:
1. **Spouse** (legal partner).
2. **Children** (biological, stepchildren, or legally adopted children).
3. **Parents** (father, mother, step-parents).
4. **Siblings** (brothers and sisters).
5. **Parents-in-Law** (father-in-law, mother-in-law).
6. **Legally Dependent Relatives** permanently residing within the employee's direct household.

#### 4.4 Remuneration, Non-Deduction & Protections
- **Full Pay & Allowances**: Bereavement Leave is fully paid, including basic pay, dearness allowance, and special duty allowances.
- **Zero Balance Deduction**: Bereavement Leave **shall not be deducted** from the employee's accrued Casual Leave (CL), Sick Leave (SL), or Earned Leave (EL) balances. It is treated as a distinct, non-chargeable compassionate entitlement.

#### 4.5 Interstate Travel & Leave Extension
- Where an employee must travel interstate or internationally for bereavement rituals, they may combine the 5 days of Bereavement Leave with **up to five (5) additional working days of accrued Earned Leave (EL)**.
- Intimation may be delivered via SMS/Email to the reporting manager and HR desk (`hrportal@nddb.coop`), with formal retrospective portal regularization permitted within 7 working days.

---

### Section 5: Earned / Privilege Leave (EL) Regulations

#### 5.1 Annual Credit & Encashment
- Employees accrue **thirty (30) days** of Earned Leave per calendar year, credited in two installments of 15 days on January 1st and July 1st.
- Accumulation is permitted up to a maximum cap of 300 days.
- Employees must submit Earned Leave requests at least fifteen (15) calendar days in advance via the HR portal.

### Section 6: Leave Application Workflow & Self-Service Portal

#### 6.1 Enterprise Portal Access
All leave transactions, balance verifications, and approvals must be processed through the official NDDB Self-Service Portal:
- **URL**: `https://hrportal.nddb.coop/leaves`
- **Authentication**: Single Sign-On (SSO) via enterprise OAuth2 / Active Directory credentials.

#### 6.2 Automated Approval Routing Rules
1. **Casual Leave ≤ 2 Days**: Automatically approved upon notification to the immediate supervisor unless an operational hold is flagged.
2. **Casual Leave = 3 Days**: Requires active digital sign-off from the immediate supervisor within 24 hours.
3. **Casual Leave > 3 Days**: Escalated to the Divisional Head / Director for exceptional approval.
4. **Sick Leave > 2 Days**: Routed automatically to HR Medical Verification Cell pending doctor's certificate upload.
5. **Bereavement Leave (up to 5 days)**: Auto-sanctioned immediately upon intimation to ensure zero administrative impediment during personal crises.

#### 6.3 REST API Interface for Autonomous Agents & Copilots
For AI Copilots, Chatbots, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers connected to NDDB systems, transactional leave submissions must invoke:

- **Endpoint**: `POST /api/leaves/apply`
- **Payload Specification**:
```json
{
  "employee_id": "string (e.g. EMP-882)",
  "leave_type": "casual | sick | earned | bereavement",
  "start_date": "YYYY-MM-DD",
  "end_date": "YYYY-MM-DD",
  "reason": "Detailed justification for leave audit trail"
}
```
- **Response Format**:
```json
{
  "success": true,
  "application_id": "LV-202609-XXXXX",
  "status": "APPROVED | PENDING",
```
